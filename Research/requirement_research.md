# Requirements Research: Internship Tracker

## Product 1: Simplify

**Website:** https://simplify.jobs/

* **Research focus:** How students keep track of their job applications and make applying for jobs easier.
* **Observations:** Simplify helps users keep their job applications organized in one place. Users can see their application statuses, dates, contacts, and what they need to do next. It also has an autofill feature that helps users save time when applying for jobs.
* **Ideas for our product:** We want to make it easy for students to add and manage their internship applications. Each application will include the company name, position, application date, deadline, and status. We could also add a field for the next step so users know what they need to do.
* **Evidence:** Screenshots are saved in the `screenshots` folder.

## Product 2: Huntr

**Website:** https://huntr.co/

* **Research focus:** How users organize their applications and keep track of where they are in the job search process.
* **Observations:** Huntr lets users organize job applications based on their current status. Users can also save job descriptions, add notes, keep track of recruiter contacts, and manage interview details. This makes it easier to find important information without having to look through different websites or documents.
* **Ideas for our product:** We could let students save notes and job descriptions with each application. We also want to make it easy to update application statuses so users can quickly see which companies they have applied to and which ones have contacted them.
* **Evidence:** Screenshots are saved in the `screenshots` folder.

## Product 3: Teal

**Website:** https://www.tealhq.com/

* **Research focus:** How users organize their job search and keep track of their progress.
* **Observations:** Teal helps users track their applications, update statuses, save notes, and manage recruiter contacts. It also gives users a way to see how their job search is going and keep track of follow-up tasks.
* **Ideas for our product:** We could add a dashboard that shows how many applications are submitted, how many interviews users have, and whether they have received any offers. This would help students understand their progress without having to count everything themselves. We could also add reminders for deadlines and interviews later.
* **Evidence:** Screenshots are saved in the `screenshots` folder.

## What We Learned

After looking at these three websites, we realized that they all help users keep their job search by organizing their jobs all in one place. For our project, we want to focus on making internship applications easy to manage, keeping track of deadlines and statuses, and showing students their progress on a dashboard. We will start with these basic features and consider adding things like reminders, AI bot and recruiter notes later.
